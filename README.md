# 🍫 Product Sales Dashboard

An interactive sales dashboard analyzing the performance of a chocolate and confectionery business across shipping modes, regions, product divisions, cities, and years (2021-2024).

## Overview
The dashboard tracks four headline KPIs:
- **Total Sales:** $725.5K
- **Average Sales:** $71.17
- **Order Count:** 10,194
- **Quantity Sold:** 38,654

## Features
- **KPI header** with total sales, average sales, order count and quantity sold
- **Slicers:** Factory, Ship Mode, Country/Region, Year
- **Donut chart:** sales share by ship mode
- **Donut chart:** sales share by region
- **Pie chart:** sales share by product division
- **Bar chart:** top 10 cities by revenue
- **Column chart:** top 5 products by revenue
- **Line chart:** yearly revenue trend

## Key Insights
- **Standard Class** is the most used ship mode (61% of sales).
- The **Pacific** region leads sales at 33%, followed by Atlantic (29%).
- The **Chocolate** division drives 92.4% of total sales.
- **New York City** is the top city ($63.9K), followed by Los Angeles ($53.0K).
- **Wonka Bar - Triple Dazzle Caramel** is the top product ($144.4K).
- Revenue grew from $147.7K in 2022 to a peak of **$238.1K in 2024**.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers)

## How to Use
1. Download the `.xlsx` file from this repository.
2. Open it in Excel.
3. Use the Factory, Ship Mode, Country/Region and Year slicers to filter all charts.

## Recommendations
- Grow the Sugar and Other divisions to reduce reliance on Chocolate.
- Promote faster shipping options (First Class, Same Day) to boost premium revenue.
- Expand marketing in lower-performing cities and regions.
- Keep investing in top products like Triple Dazzle Caramel.
